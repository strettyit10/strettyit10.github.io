How to use HoyaDocs

HoyaDocs is your one-stop shop to supplement, test, and consolidate what you're already learning — whether that's in-house lectures, BnB, Pathoma, Physeo, Sketchy, the BK Anki deck, ANKing, or anything else.

It is NOT meant to teach you the content. Think of it as a tailored aid that sits on top of whatever primary sources you're using.

Recommended Workflow

One full pass through a lecture. Click any step to expand the details.

01 Review the Lecture Guide ›
The night before content is introduced, skim the lecture guide to prime your brain for what's coming. You're not memorizing — you're building scaffolding.
02a Watch the 3rd-Party Resource ›
Before (or instead of) the in-house lecture, watch the mapped 3rd-party video — BnB, Bootcamp, Pathoma, Physeo, or Sketchy. This primes you for the lecture content and gives you the Step 1 framing upfront.
02b Watch the In-House Lecture ›
Minimize stop time. Your notes are the MNTS and the lecture guide — let the lecturer build the narrative without constantly pausing to write.
03 Complete the Recall Drill ›
Ideally the same day as Step 2. A no-frills, fill-in-the-blank style drill over the high-yield content from the lecture guide. Quick, low-stakes, forces retrieval.
04 Answer Lecture Guide MCQs & Q&A ›
Use the Recall Drill and Lecture Guide as your reference, then work through the embedded MCQs and Q&A. This is where passive recognition turns into active application.
05 Reinforce via the 3 Active Drill Hubs ›
Three drill hubs per lecture, each progressively harder — designed as an alternative or supportive track to Anki while still hitting spaced repetition. The progression concludes with the lecture's mapped UWorld QIDs.
06 Board-Style Practice ›
Shift from lecture-based reinforcement to exam-style, system-based application. Questions are USMLE Step 1 style — a mix of AI-generated stems, 3rd-party questions, and items pulled from the free USMLE 120 exams.
07 Formative Feedback ›
Head over to Canvas and complete any formative feedback — final tune-up before the block exam. This closes the loop and flags anything still soft.

Practice Problem Resources

The drill system is staged on purpose. Hub 1 reinforces what was in lecture. Hub 2 bridges lecture to Step 1. Hub 3 tests you like the boards will. Work them in order.

Core Concepts

Step 1 Extensions

Where to Find What

Quick reference — if you're looking for a specific thing, this is the shortest path to it.

🔎
"I want a guided overview of today's lecture." Block home page → Lecture Study Guides. Pick the lecture. The guide reads like a clean study note with embedded questions.
🧠
"I want to actively test recall after lecture." Block home page → Active Drills → pick the lecture → Hub 1. Flashcards and fill-in-the-blank.
📚
"I want Step 1 framing and clinical correlates." Same lecture page → Hub 2. Combines mapped 3rd-party resources with clinical correlates, HY notes, and trap drills.
🩺
"I want to feel what Step 1 vignettes are like." Same lecture page → Hub 3. Timed exam blocks with explanations and a score dashboard.
🗂
"I want to know what BnB / Bootcamp / Pathoma / Physeo / Sketchy covers this lecture." Block home page → 3rd-Party Mapping. Side-by-side matrix for every lecture in the block.
⭐
"I want the UWorld QIDs or Anki tags for a lecture." Block home page → Lecture Resources → per-lecture card. UWorld QIDs and Anki tags are listed directly.
