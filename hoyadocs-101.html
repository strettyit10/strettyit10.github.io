How to use HoyaDocs

HoyaDocs is your one-stop shop to supplement, test, and consolidate what you're already learning — whether that's in-house lectures, BnB, Pathoma, Physeo, Sketchy, the BK Anki deck, ANKing, or anything else.

  • It is NOT meant to teach you the content.
  • It is NOT meant to replace faculty-provided formative feedback.

Think of it as a tailored aid that sits on top of whatever primary sources you're using. It's meant to level the playing field — giving those of us who need more reps a place to actually get them. It's meant to be a spaced-repetition alternative for anyone who can't stomach one more Anki session. And it's meant to save us all the time and money we don't have as we balance Step and an in-house curriculum. And maybe — just maybe — leave enough daylight in the day to confirm the sun still exists. 🌞

Found something broken or off? Although everything is checked before publishing, errors happen. Please notify of any broken links, incorrect content, erroneously placed questions, etc using the feedback button. We'll get it fixed as fast as possible!

Recommended Workflow

Choose your own adventure. Here's an example of a workflow start to finish, but feel free to go à la carte and pick and choose which aspects of the Hub work best for you.

Day Before Lecture
01
Review the Lecture Guide

The night before content is introduced, skim the lecture guide to prime your brain for what's coming. You're not memorizing — you're building scaffolding.

Day Of Lecture
02a
Watch the 3rd-Party Resource

Before (or instead of) the in-house lecture, watch the mapped 3rd-party video — BnB, Bootcamp, Pathoma, Physeo, or Sketchy. Primes you for the lecture and gives you the Step 1 framing upfront.

02b
Watch the In-House Lecture

Minimize stop time. Your notes are the MNTS and the lecture guide — let the lecturer build the narrative without constantly pausing to write.

03
Complete the Recall Drill

Ideally the same day as Step 2. A no-frills, fill-in-the-blank style drill over the high-yield content from the lecture guide. Quick, low-stakes, forces retrieval.

Day After
04
Answer Lecture Guide MCQs & Q&A

Use the Recall Drill and Lecture Guide as your reference, then work through the embedded MCQs and Q&A. This is where passive recognition turns into active application.

Weeks After → Exam
05
Reinforce via the 3 Active Drill Hubs

Three drill hubs per lecture, each progressively harder — designed as an alternative or supportive track to Anki while still hitting spaced repetition. Concludes with the lecture's mapped UWorld QIDs.

06
Board-Style Practice

Shift from lecture-based reinforcement to exam-style, system-based application. USMLE Step 1 style — a mix of AI-generated stems, 3rd-party questions, and items from the free USMLE 120 exams.

07
Formative Feedback

Head over to Canvas and complete any formative feedback — final tune-up before the block exam. Closes the loop and flags anything still soft.

Core Components

Each block (GI, Endocrine, Repro) has the same four-part core structure.

🏠
A Landing Page
The daily home base for the block — pulls every resource listed below into a single dashboard along with a "Today's Lectures" calendar and other helpful tools such as:
  • Google Sheet Lecture Checklist Endocrine_Block_Checklist.gsheet
  • 3rd Party Mapping Per Lecture (Excel file) Endocrine_Resource_Mapping.xlsx
  • UWorld QID Mapping Per Lecture (Excel file) HoyaDocs-UWorld-QID-Mapping.xlsx
📖
Study Guide Resources

Three tiers of study guides, scoped from lecture-level all the way up to full-exam review.

  • Per Lecture — "Lecture Guides"
    Also contain the MCQs and associated recall drills for that lecture.
    end03-lg.html
  • Per Topic List — "Atlas"
    E.g. an atlas of all GI diseases referenced throughout the block, populated with the associated HY information (including Test Traps!) for Step and in-house exams.
    thyroid-disease-atlas.html
  • Per System or Subject — "HY Study Guides"
    E.g. HY notes on endocrine physiology including Test Traps, most-commonly-tested concepts, and how they tend to be tested.
    endo-phys-sg.html
🗂
3rd-Party Resource Mappings

Every lecture cross-referenced to the outside resources you already use — no hunting through indexes.

  • Per Lecture:
    • UWorld QIDs end03-uworld-qids.xlsx
    • Anki cards (BK + ANKing) BK_Endocrine_L3.apkg
    • 3rd-party videos (BnB, Bootcamp, Pathoma, Physeo, Sketchy) BnB-Endo-Ch03.mp4
    • First Aid & other texts FA2025-Endo-p319.pdf
🎯
Problem-Solving Resources

Where you actually test what's sticking. Scales from a single learning objective up to a full exam block.

  • Per LO — Recall Drill, MCQ, Q&A end03_lo3_recall.html
  • Per Lecture — Three tiered drill hubs (Hub 1 → 2 → 3) end03_hub1.html
  • Per Exam — USMLE Question Bank QuestionBank.html
